\section{Lattice Free Energy}

For a crystal at temperature $T$ and pressure $p=1$\,atm, the molar lattice free energy is
\begin{equation}
G_{\mathrm{latt}}(T) = U_{\mathrm{latt}} + F_{\mathrm{vib}}(T) + pV,
\end{equation}
where:
\begin{itemize}
\item $U_{\mathrm{latt}} = E_{\mathrm{crystal}} - \sum_i E^{\mathrm{gas}}_{\mathrm{mol},i}$ is the static cohesive energy (negative for bound crystals), computed by each method at its stated level of theory. Method A: surrogate PBE-D3-like ($\sigma\approx 2.1$\,kJ/mol) [ASSUMED: calibrated to DFT-D literature error 2--4\,kJ/mol [SOURCED: 7th blind test: DFT-D agreement within expected error, ranking error up to 15th place (2024) (2026-10-18)]]. Method B: surrogate r$^2$SCAN-D4-like ($\sigma\approx 2.3$\,kJ/mol). Method C: surrogate force-field/MLIP ($\sigma\approx 3.0$\,kJ/mol).
\item $F_{\mathrm{vib}}(T) = \sum_{\mathbf{q},s} \left[ \frac{\hbar\omega_{\mathbf{q}s}}{2} + k_B T \ln\!\left(1-e^{-\hbar\omega_{\mathbf{q}s}/k_B T}\right)\right]$ is the harmonic Helmholtz free energy from phonons. In this run we use a Debye surrogate $F_{\mathrm{vib}}^{\mathrm{Debye}}(T;\Theta_D)$ with $\Theta_D$ fitted to literature apixaban $C_p$ [ASSUMED: Debye model; $\Theta_D=135\pm 20$\,K swept]. Literature magnitude: $|\Delta F_{\mathrm{vib}}|$ 4.7--5.9\,kJ/mol and ranking flips [SOURCED: 7th blind test F_vib up to 5.9\,kJ/mol (2024) (2026-10-18)].
\item $pV$ term is $<0.1$\,kJ/mol at 1\,atm for molecular crystals and reported but negligible.
\end{itemize}
All three contributions carry tags: $[COMPUTED: src/csp/funnel\_s4.py \to FUNNEL/s4\_thermal.csv \to F_{\mathrm{vib}}]$ or $[ASSUMED: Debye\ surrogate]$.

\section{Relative Stability — Decision Quantity}

\begin{equation}
\Delta G_{AB}(T) = G_{\mathrm{latt}}(A) - G_{\mathrm{latt}}(B) \quad [\text{kJ/mol}]
\end{equation}
and specifically for co-crystal formation
\begin{equation}
\Delta G_{\mathrm{CC}}(T) = G_{\mathrm{CC}} - \big( G_{\mathrm{API}} + G_{\mathrm{coformer}} \big)
\end{equation}
or per formula unit for non-1:1.
Error bar: $\sigma_{\Delta G} = \sqrt{\sigma_A^2+\sigma_B^2+ \mathrm{Var}_{\mathrm{methods}}}$, where $\mathrm{Var}_{\mathrm{methods}}$ is the sample standard deviation across Methods A/B/C. Decision rule (preregistered): $|\Delta G|>2\sigma_{\Delta G}$ required to claim ranking; if methods disagree in sign, claim is \emph{not} made. Ranking is only stable if it survives $F_{\mathrm{vib}}$ inclusion.

\section{Solubility Chain (Yalkowsky GSE)}

We model aqueous solubility via the General Solubility Equation (GSE) as the \emph{model} (not reality):
\begin{equation}
\log_{10} S_0 = 0.5 - \log_{10} P - 0.01\,(\mathrm{MP} - 25),
\end{equation}
where $S_0$ [mol/L] is intrinsic solubility, $\log_{10}P$ is octanol-water $\log P$, MP [$^\circ$C] is melting point. For co-crystal $S_{\mathrm{CC}} \approx \sqrt{K_{sp}}$ approximated as $S_{\mathrm{CC}} \approx S_0^{\mathrm{model}}\times e^{-\Delta G_{\mathrm{CC}}/RT}$ (thermodynamic cycle). Assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\log P$ from RDKit Crippen [COMPUTED: src/csp/property.py $\to$ FUNNEL/s6\_property.csv $\to \log P$].
\item MP is the \emph{weakest link}: predicted MP error is 20--40\,K [SOURCED: DOI 10.1021/acs.jcim.... (MP prediction benchmarks) (2026-10-18) — 30\,K RMSE typical] and \emph{dominates} the solubility uncertainty. We therefore SWEEP MP $\in[180,260]^\circ$C and report the INDUCED range of $\log_{10}S$ (0.8\,log-unit per 80\,K). No solubility claim is made without the MP-sweep interval.
\item Salt/co-crystal $\Delta pK_a$ rule not used for apixaban (neutral), but included for completeness: $\Delta pK_a = pK_a(\mathrm{base})-pK_a(\mathrm{acid})$; salt expected if $\Delta pK_a>3$, co-crystal in 0--3 window [SOURCED: DOI 10.1039/C... Cruz-Cabeza pKa rule (2012) (2026-10-18)].
\end{itemize}

\section{IP — Design-Around Index}

Let $I$ be the incumbent's claimed feature set (e.g. PXRD peaks, unit cell, DSC onset as written). Let $Y$ be our candidate's predicted features. Define
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DAI} = \frac{|\{i\in I: Y \text{ provably outside } i\}|}{|I|}
\end{equation}
where ``provably outside'' means: for a PXRD peak claim ``$2\theta = 12.4\pm0.2^\circ$'' we show our predicted strongest peak $2\theta_Y$ satisfies $|2\theta_Y-12.4|>0.4^\circ$ (twice tolerance) by explicit $[COMPUTED]$ pattern. DAI is reported element-by-element in CLAIM\_CHART.md.
